    <!-- 10 Signs -->
    <section class="section">
        <h2 class="section-title">10 Warning Signs It's Time to Upgrade</h2>

        <div class="grid">

            <div class="info-box">
                <h3>📞 1. Your Phone System Is Outdated</h3>
                <p>Older telephone systems are expensive to maintain, difficult to upgrade, and lack the flexibility modern businesses need.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="info-box">
                <h3>📵 2. You're Missing Customer Calls</h3>
                <p>Missed calls mean missed opportunities. Modern telecoms systems use call routing, queues and auto attendants to keep customers connected.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="info-box">
                <h3>💷 3. Phone Costs Keep Increasing</h3>
                <p>High call charges, maintenance fees and ageing hardware can cost your business thousands over time.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="info-box">
                <h3>📈 4. Your Business Has Outgrown Its System</h3>
                <p>Adding new users, extensions or office locations shouldn't require expensive hardware upgrades.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="info-box">
                <h3>🏡 5. You Support Hybrid Working</h3>
                <p>Your team should be able to answer business calls from home, the office or while travelling.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="info-box">
                <h3>⏳ 6. Customers Wait Too Long</h3>
                <p>Long hold times and poor call routing can damage customer satisfaction and your reputation.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="info-box">
                <h3>⚙️ 7. Your System Is Difficult to Manage</h3>
                <p>If every simple change requires an engineer visit, your telecoms solution is costing you time and money.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="info-box">
                <h3>💬 8. Multiple Communication Platforms</h3>
                <p>Using separate apps for calls, meetings and messaging creates unnecessary complexity.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="info-box">
                <h3>🔊 9. Poor Call Quality</h3>
                <p>Echo, dropped calls, delays and poor audio all reduce professionalism and productivity.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="info-box">
                <h3>🤝 10. Your Provider Isn't Supporting Growth</h3>
                <p>Your telecoms provider should offer advice, proactive support and solutions that help your business succeed.</p>
            </div>

        </div>
    </section>
